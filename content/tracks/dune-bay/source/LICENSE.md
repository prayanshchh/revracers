# Source data licences

## osm.json

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database
License (ODbL) 1.0: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

Extracted through the Overpass API by `tools/track/dunebay_fetch.py`: the
circuit relation, its start line node, and the buildings, roads, barriers,
land use, car parks, leisure areas and trees in the box around the venue.
`track.json`, `dune-bay.xml`, `circuit-map.json`, `racing-line.json`,
`venue.json` and `trees.bin` are Produced Works derived from this database
and must keep this attribution.

## survey.json and surface.json

Track edge lines and run-off spans measured from aerial imagery
(Source: Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User Community).
Only the measured coordinates are stored; no imagery is redistributed.
